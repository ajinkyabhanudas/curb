# Five questions

**Q1. Before the report is shown.**
"Tell me about the last time you had to check whether a vendor, or your own team, kept to a clause about which AI providers could see customer data. What did you look at, and how long did it take?"
Follow-ups if needed: Who asked for it? What did you hand over at the end? What was the hardest part?

**Q2. After showing `sample-report-fail.md`.**
"In your own words, what does this tell you? What does it not tell you?"
Do not point at the "does not establish" lines. Note which ones they name unprompted.

**Q3. Reliance.**
"Which part would you rely on, and which would you ignore? What would you need that is not here?"
Follow-up: "When it says UNKNOWN, what would you do next?"

**Q4. Trust.**
"Who else would have to trust this output besides you (legal, security, the customer, an auditor), and what would they ask first?"

**Q5. Use.**
"If this existed for your next review, what would you do with it? What would stop you from using it?"
Follow-up: "What would you stop doing if it worked?"

## Do not ask

- "Would you use it?" or "Do you like it?" (answers say little). Q5 asks what they would do and what blocks them.
- Anything that names a real company's code or says a particular vendor failed. The samples are synthetic.
